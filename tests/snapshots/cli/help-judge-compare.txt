Usage: root judge-compare [OPTIONS] {fragment}

 Agreement of the judge's file with the decided adjudication file, overall and by how each span was
 decided (inferred), and the WER between the decided reference and one resolved from the judged
 file. Prints span texts: never use it on a sealed fragment.
